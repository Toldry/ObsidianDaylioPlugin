## Reading Notes: Piranesi — Susanna Clarke

About halfway through. The conceit is extraordinary: a man living in a vast, labyrinthine House filled with statues and tidal halls, keeping careful notes about the Tides and the Statues and his single companion, the Other.

The prose has the clinical precision of someone trying to hold reality together through documentation. Which is exactly what journaling is, I suppose.
